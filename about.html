What Bradenton Slab Leak Pros Does

A leak under your slab is stressful enough without a second problem: finding someone who can actually locate it. We exist to solve that second problem. Based at 2200-2298 11th Ave W in Bradenton, Florida, we connect homeowners across Bradenton ZIPs 34201 to 34212, plus the surrounding Manatee County and island communities, with independent plumbing contractors equipped for leak detection and slab repair.

How a Service Call Works

It starts with your call and a description of the signs: the warm floor, the meter, the bill. That description determines priority, because an actively flooding slab jumps the route. The contractor arrives, pressure tests the system, pinpoints the leak, and shows you exactly where it is. You approve a written plan. The repair is completed and pressure tested before the contractor leaves.
