Questions People Ask When the Meter Won't Stop

Short answers for the first few minutes.

What should I do right now if I suspect a slab leak?

Check your water meter: if the dial or leak indicator moves while nothing in the house is using water, you likely have a leak. Note any warm floor spots, then call (941) 307-1712 and describe the signs. A technician will talk you through the next steps.

How do you find a leak under concrete without tearing up my floors?

Acoustic listening equipment hears pressurized water escaping under the slab, and thermal imaging shows the warm trail a hot line leaves. Together they narrow the leak to a small area before anything is opened.

Can a slab leak really damage my foundation?

Yes. A leak under steady pressure washes sand and soil out from beneath the slab, and the concrete settles into the void. Finding the leak early is the cheapest foundation protection there is.

Do you cover all of Bradenton and the surrounding areas?

Yes. Bradenton itself, plus Sarasota, Palmetto, Parrish, Lakewood Ranch, Ellenton, Anna Maria, Holmes Beach, Bradenton Beach, and Cortez.

Will the repair be explained before any work starts?

Always. You get the findings in plain language, the repair options that fit your home, and a written plan before any work begins.

What signs should make me call sooner rather than later?

A warm floor spot, a water bill that jumped in a dry month, the sound of running water with everything off, or new cracks in tile and drywall. Any one of them earns a meter test.
